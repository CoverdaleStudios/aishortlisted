# Bin Caddie (Sunshine Coast, QLD): Marketing, Brand, Online Presence and Customer Reputation

Research date: 10 October 2026. Method: web search, a direct fetch of the live site (bincaddie.com.au), and source code inspection of its JavaScript bundles. The site is a single-page React app, so most of the copy, pricing and links sit in the JS files rather than the HTML. The site's sitemap.xml shows every page with lastmod 2026-10-09, so the copy below is the live version as of this date.

## What is Bin Caddie's tagline/value proposition and who are they targeting?

### Takeaway
Bin Caddie sells itself as a "hands off" B2B-style bin management service for holiday rentals, Airbnb hosts, property managers and body corporates/strata from Caloundra to Noosa. Its hero line is "Hands Off Bin Support." Its main differentiators are photo-verified proof of service, scheduling timed to council collections, no lock-in contracts, and being family owned and local. Elderly and mobility-limited residents are a secondary audience, mentioned on the service pages but not on the homepage.

### Cited Findings
**Tagline and core messaging (live site, Oct 2026)**
- The homepage hero is "Hands Off Bin Support." The site says Bin Caddie puts bins out and in each week, keeps them clean and clears overflow "so a property runs itself." — [bincaddie.com.au](https://bincaddie.com.au/)
- Homepage trust badges read "Family Owned", "Satisfaction Guaranteed" and "Sunshine Coast". — [bincaddie.com.au](https://bincaddie.com.au/)
- The recurring brand line is "take bins off your to do list". The homepage has "Watch how we take bins off your to do list" and the closing CTA "Ready to Take Bins Off Your To Do List?", and the About page also uses the phrase. — [bincaddie.com.au](https://bincaddie.com.au/)
- About page headline: "The Sunshine Coast's Trusted Bin Management Team". It continues: "Managing bins is more complex than it sounds — but that's exactly what Bin Caddie was built to solve." — [bincaddie.com.au/about](https://bincaddie.com.au/about) (text taken from the site JS bundle)
- Brand story: "Bin Caddie is a local family run business with over a decade of experience in property management across the Whitsundays." An earlier indexed version of the About page said the business began in "caretaker services for Whitsundays holiday homes" and had built a following in the Airbnb community. — [bincaddie.com.au/about](https://bincaddie.com.au/about); earlier version indexed at [www.bincaddie.com.au/about-us](https://www.bincaddie.com.au/about-us), which now returns 404
- The three proof points on the About page are local knowledge ("We know every Sunshine Coast collection schedule and suburb, so your bins are always out on time and back in before guests arrive"), "Photo Verified Every Visit" ("clear, timestamped proof the job was done") and "Hassle Free Communication" ("Once you're set up, everything runs in the background"). — [bincaddie.com.au/about](https://bincaddie.com.au/about)

**Services and offer structure**
- There are three services: Bin Placement (bins out and in), Bin Cleaning and Rubbish Removal. Bin placement is sold with "no lock-in contracts and you can cancel anytime" and photographic proof of each service. — [bincaddie.com.au](https://bincaddie.com.au/)
- Bin placement is priced from $80/month ex GST for up to 4 residential wheelie bins (240L & 360L), with 2 visits per week. The FAQ says "Commercial bin placement also starts from $80 per month." The on-page price cards list "Holiday Home – $80 per month" (tags: Residential, Domestic, Airbnb, Holiday Home) and "Body Corporate – From $100 per month" (tags: Complex, Body Corporate, Strata, Hotel / Resort). The FAQ ($80 commercial) and the price card ($100 body corporate) do not quite agree. — [bincaddie.com.au/bin-placement](https://bincaddie.com.au/bin-placement)
- Bin cleaning is "Deep clean & sanitise, starting from just $17." The instant-quote calculator shows: 1 small bin at $17 fortnightly, $20 every 4 weeks, $25 quarterly or $30 once-off; 2 bins at $25/$30/$45/$60; large 660–1100L bins at $30/$35/$50/$70 each; all ex GST. Large orders get the message "please contact us directly to receive a discounted rate." Cleaning quotes are limited to postcodes 4551–4568, 4572, 4573 and 4575. — [bincaddie.com.au/bin-cleaning](https://bincaddie.com.au/bin-cleaning) (quote calculator in the site JS)
- Bin cleaning is delivered in partnership with Turbo's Wheelie Clean, an established local bin cleaner. The cleaning page is titled "Bin Cleaning with Turbo's Wheelie Clean | Bin Caddie" and has a section headed "Two Local Teams, One Service". It reuses Turbo's own claims ("Throughout our 7 years of operation…", "100% satisfaction guarantee, if your bin isn't clean enough, we'll be back") and Turbo's before/after photos and video. — [bincaddie.com.au/bin-cleaning](https://bincaddie.com.au/bin-cleaning); partner site [turboswheelieclean.com.au](https://turboswheelieclean.com.au/services/commercial-customers/)
- Rubbish removal is positioned as "no skips, no hassle", with "Same day available" and a "fast, upfront quote, no hidden fees". It covers furniture, whitegoods, renovation debris, green waste, end-of-tenancy cleanouts and "Holiday Rental Bin Emptying" changeover services with "Photo confirmation sent after every visit". — [bincaddie.com.au/rubbish-removal](https://bincaddie.com.au/rubbish-removal)
- Earlier indexed pages included a "Bin2Kerb" brand name for bin placement and a "Garage Clean" service, which suggests the service line has been narrowed and renamed since. — [www.bincaddie.com.au/bin-placement-bin2kerb](https://www.bincaddie.com.au/bin-placement-bin2kerb) and [www.bincaddie.com.au/garage-clean](https://www.bincaddie.com.au/garage-clean), both appearing in search indexes; bin-placement-bin2kerb returned 404 on 10 Oct 2026
- Accepted payment methods: "Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer". The site promises "We respond to all enquiries within 24 hours." — [bincaddie.com.au/contact](https://bincaddie.com.au/contact)

**Target customers**
- The homepage says it is "Built for holiday rentals and body corporates". It lists property managers, co-hosts, body corporates, facilities managers, holiday rental agencies, strata managers, building managers, Airbnb hosts, short stay operators, real estate agencies, resort managers and onsite managers. — [bincaddie.com.au](https://bincaddie.com.au/)
- The bin placement page adds "Elderly & Mobility Limited Residents – Residents who need a helping hand getting bins to and from the kerb". Its FAQ names "holiday rental hosts, Airbnb owners, strata and body corporate complexes, property managers, and elderly or mobility limited residents". — [bincaddie.com.au/bin-placement](https://bincaddie.com.au/bin-placement)
- Busy families and general households are not called out as a segment. The only household-facing services are rubbish removal (households, garage cleanouts) and bin cleaning (residential). — [bincaddie.com.au/rubbish-removal](https://bincaddie.com.au/rubbish-removal)
- Service area copy reads "Caloundra to Noosa", listing Caloundra, Kawana, Mooloolaba, Maroochydore, Buderim, Sippy Downs, Bli Bli, Nambour, Yandina, Coolum Beach, Peregian Beach and Noosa Heads, plus "Not sure if we cover your area? Just ask!" — [bincaddie.com.au](https://bincaddie.com.au/)

**Tone of voice**
- The tone is plain, friendly and reassuring, written to stressed hosts and managers: "Never Worry About Bin Collection Again", "Guests never lift a finger, we handle it all", "We load everything up, sweep the area clean, and dispose of it all responsibly. Done." It leans on Aussie colloquial puns borrowed from the partner ("We'll get your bins wheelie clean"). There is also a guest-experience angle: dirty bins "can seriously impact your guests' experience and your property's reviews." — [bincaddie.com.au/bin-placement](https://bincaddie.com.au/bin-placement); [bincaddie.com.au/rubbish-removal](https://bincaddie.com.au/rubbish-removal); [bincaddie.com.au/bin-cleaning](https://bincaddie.com.au/bin-cleaning)

### Inferences
- Positioning is clearly B2B/B2B2C, aimed at short-stay and strata property managers. That fits the founders' stated background in Whitsundays holiday-home caretaking. Photo proof, no lock-in and timing to council collection are features chosen to win absentee owners and managers, not households.
- The service has been rebuilt and narrowed. The older site had a Bin2Kerb brand, Garage Clean and "affordable rubbish collection" pages. The October 2026 site has three core services and two new audience landing pages (/holiday-rental-bin-service, /body-corporate-bin-management).
- Bin cleaning looks white-labelled or subcontracted through Turbo's Wheelie Clean rather than done in-house. However, the bin-cleaning service page also describes "Our mobile cleaning unit", so the copy is ambiguous about who does the work.

### Gaps
- Could not view the pre-2026 site in the Wayback Machine (blocked by the environment's egress policy). The founding date, founder names and any old testimonials are unconfirmed. The site does not name the owners.
- The live site has no on-page testimonials, review widget or star ratings.

## What paid or offline marketing do they appear to use?

### Takeaway
The website is set up for paid acquisition. It has a Meta Pixel, Google Tag Manager and GA4 installed, plus a "How did you hear about us?" field whose options include "Saw our vehicle" and "Property Manager / Agency". ### Cited Findings

- After an enquiry, the post-submission form asks "How did you hear about us?" with the options `Google`, `Facebook / Instagram`, `Referral`, `Property Manager / Agency`, `Returning Customer`, `Saw our vehicle` and `Other`. — [bincaddie.com.au/contact](https://bincaddie.com.au/contact) (site JS)

## Any referral, loyalty, or promotional offers?

### Takeaway
No formal referral, loyalty or discount program is advertised. The offers are low-friction ones: free no-obligation quotes, an instant online price calculator, "from $17" cleaning and "from $80/month" placement anchors, no lock-in contracts, volume discounts on request for large bin counts, same-day rubbish removal and a satisfaction guarantee.

### Cited Findings
- "Get a Free Quote" / "free, no-obligation quote" CTAs appear across the site. — [bincaddie.com.au](https://bincaddie.com.au/); [bincaddie.com.au/contact](https://bincaddie.com.au/contact)
- "No lock-in contracts and you can cancel anytime" applies to bin placement. — [bincaddie.com.au](https://bincaddie.com.au/)
- "Deep clean & sanitise, starting from just $17". Subscription frequencies (fortnightly, 4-weekly, quarterly) are cheaper per clean than a once-off. Large bin counts get "please contact us directly to receive a discounted rate." — [bincaddie.com.au/bin-cleaning](https://bincaddie.com.au/bin-cleaning)
- Guarantees: "Satisfaction Guaranteed" and "100% satisfaction guarantee, if your bin isn't clean enough, we'll be back". — [bincaddie.com.au](https://bincaddie.com.au/); [bincaddie.com.au/bin-cleaning](https://bincaddie.com.au/bin-cleaning)

### Inferences
