# Bin Caddie: Competitive Landscape and Market Context (Sunshine Coast and Noosa, QLD)

Research date: 10 October 2026. All prices are as displayed on websites when fetched or indexed. Several competitor pages were indexed 8 to 25 months ago, so their prices may be out of date (flagged where known). Prices are in AUD.

## Who are the main competitors to Bin Caddie on the Sunshine Coast/Noosa and what do they charge?

### Takeaway
The local market splits into two groups. The first is **bin-cleaning-only operators**, priced at about $10 to $20 per bin per clean: Turbo's Wheelie Clean (Sunshine Coast based, about 81 reviews at 4.7 stars), Coastal Bin Cleaning, ChuteOff, and a small Facebook operator, Wheelie Kleen (Mons). The second is **dedicated bin valet (put-out/bring-in) services**, where searches found no Sunshine Coast or Noosa operator besides Bin Caddie. The nearest comparable valet business is The Bin Butler on the Gold Coast, which charges $160/month inc. GST.

### Inferences
- In bin valet, Bin Caddie appears to have **no visible dedicated local competitor**. Its realistic substitutes are holiday-rental property managers or cleaners doing bins in-house, neighbours or family, and the cleaning operators that informally return bins to the house.
- In bin cleaning, Bin Caddie's "from $17" starting price is **above** the cheapest local advertised rates (ChuteOff $10–15; Turbo's from $15; Coastal Bin Cleaning about $10 per bin monthly if still current). Bin Caddie therefore competes on bundling and on serving holiday rentals and strata, not on price.
- Bin Caddie's $80/month ex GST ($88 inc. GST) holiday-home valet is **about 45% cheaper** than The Bin Butler's Gold Coast $160 inc. GST. Bin Butler's price includes regular cleaning, so the two are not a like-for-like comparison.
- The equivalent of $80/month is roughly $18–20 per weekly bin day for placement alone.
- Turbo's Wheelie Clean is the most established local operator, with a review base Bin Caddie lacks. If Turbo's added put-out/bring-in, or if a Gold Coast valet such as The Bin Butler expanded north, that would be the most direct threat.

## How does Bin Caddie differentiate?

### Takeaway
Bin Caddie positions itself as a **B2B "hands-off" bin management service for holiday rentals, Airbnb hosts, property managers and body corporates/strata**. It bundles weekly put-out/bring-in, cleaning and rubbish removal, with photo proof of each visit and no lock-in contracts. Local competitors sell bin cleaning to householders on a per-bin basis.

### Cited Findings
- Bin Caddie offers weekly placement plus cleaning plus rubbish removal. Its target list includes property managers, co-hosts, body corporates, facilities and strata managers, Airbnb hosts, resort and onsite managers, and real estate agencies — [Bin Caddie home](https://www.bincaddie.com.au/)
- Photo of every placement, flexible schedule, coordination of bins for every unit in multi-unit complexes, no lock-in, and a 2-week free trial — [Bin Caddie bin placement](https://www.bincaddie.com.au/bin-placement)
- Time- and location-stamped proof-of-service photos for cleaning, with a re-clean guarantee — [Bin Caddie bin cleaning](https://www.bincaddie.com.au/bin-cleaning)
- Claims local knowledge of "every council collection schedule and suburb" and over a decade of property management in the Whitsundays — [Bin Caddie about](https://www.bincaddie.com.au/about)
- Competitor comparison:
  - Turbo's requires bins at the kerb and charges 50% if they are not out, with no valet — [Turbo's FAQ](https://turboswheelieclean.com.au/frequently-asked-questions/)
  - ChuteOff offers cleaning only — [ChuteOff](https://www.chuteoff.com.au/bin-cleaning/)
  - The Bin Butler sets a 3-month minimum term — [The Bin Butler](https://thebinbutler.com/bin-valet/)

### Inferences
- Main points of difference:
  - (1) Valet as the core product, since cleaners depend on bins already being at the kerb.
  - (2) Flat monthly property pricing that suits property managers' budgeting.
  - (3) Photo audit trail, useful for absentee owners and body corporate committees.
  - (4) No lock-in, against The Bin Butler's 3-month minimum.
  - (5) One provider for bins plus overflow and hard rubbish.
- Weaknesses compared with local cleaners:
  - Higher entry cleaning price (from $17).
  - No visible review base.
  - Business looks young (site © 2024).
- Bin Caddie lists elderly and mobility-limited residents as a target, but its packaging and messaging centre on holiday rentals and strata. A retiree-focused residential tier is not evident.

### Gaps
- Whether Bin Caddie uses water recapture or a truck versus a trailer (the page mentions a mobile unit with its own water supply, but not recapture).
- Bin Caddie's actual customer mix and retention.

## What are the relevant council waste collection arrangements and recent/upcoming changes (e.g. FOGO, weekly vs fortnightly) that affect demand?

### Takeaway
Both councils run a **weekly general waste, fortnightly recycling and fortnightly garden organics** system, with no change to weekly general waste announced. As of October 2026, **household FOGO has not been rolled out** in either council. Sunshine Coast describes garden organics as "the first step" toward FOGO but gives no date. A Council of Mayors (SEQ) food-organics trial is running at retirement or multi-unit sites (Sunshine Coast) and Peppers Noosa Resort (Noosa) through 2026–2027.

### Cited Findings
**Sunshine Coast Council**
- Weekly domestic general waste, fortnightly recycling, fortnightly garden organics. The page was last updated 29 September 2026. A "Food organics trial" page is listed in the section menu — [SCC bin collection services](https://www.sunshinecoast.qld.gov.au/living-and-community/waste-and-recycling/bin-collection-services)
- General waste bin lids are changing **from green to red** "over time" to meet Australian standards. Standard 240L bin, maximum 80kg. Fee is included in rates. Extra collections are charged, and tenants must pre-pay — [SCC general waste](https://www.sunshinecoast.qld.gov.au/living-and-community/waste-and-recycling/bin-collection-services/general-waste-bin-collection)
- Expanded garden organics service started **11 July 2022**:
  - Compulsory for single-unit dwellings, except lots under 300m² or over 5000m², or households that compost or use a gardener (exemption on application).
  - **$50.50/year**, emptied fortnightly on alternate weeks to recycling. Apartments, townhouses and duplexes can opt in.
  - Council describes it as "the first step towards a FOGO service" while it investigates processing facilities. No timing given.
  - [SCC garden organics FAQ](https://www.sunshinecoast.qld.gov.au/living-and-community/waste-and-recycling/bin-collection-services/garden-waste-bin-collection/frequently-asked-questions)
- Earlier 2021 reporting: before the expansion, about 28% of ratepayers used the opt-in green bin, and council had hoped FOGO might follow by about 2025 (subject to a business case) — [Sunshine Coast News, Aug 2021](https://www.sunshinecoastnews.com.au/2021/08/06/green-waste-bin-sunshine-coast-council/) (via search summary)
- Food organics trial:
  - Runs **April 2026 to December 2027** at two Sunshine Coast retirement communities (multi-unit).
  - Participants get a kitchen caddy, compostable liners and a FOGO bin. No other properties are included.
  - Its purpose is to inform a later regional expansion.
  - Led by the Council of Mayors (SEQ) and funded by the Queensland Government. Brisbane, Gold Coast and Noosa also participate.
  - [Sunshine Coast News, 4 Mar 2026](https://www.sunshinecoastnews.com.au/2026/03/04/bin-to-soil-retirement-villages-lead-the-way/)
- Bins out before 6am, trucks run 6am–6pm, bins within 0.5m of the roadside — [SCC recycling bin collection](https://www.sunshinecoast.qld.gov.au/living-and-community/waste-and-recycling/bin-collection-services/recycling-bin-collection) (via search summary)
- SCC runs an automated (underground/pneumatic) waste collection system for developments, relevant to the Maroochydore City Centre — [SCC automated waste collection system](https://www.sunshinecoast.qld.gov.au/living-and-community/waste-and-recycling/automated-waste-collection-system/developers-designers-and-builders) (title only; not fetched)

**Noosa Shire Council**
- 240L general waste with red or green lid (weekly). 240L recycling, yellow lid (fortnightly, same day as general waste). 240L garden waste, lime lid (alternate fortnight). 360L bins available. Paid extra casual collections.
- Placement rules: out before 6am, 300mm apart, under 80kg, handles toward the property, **remove from kerb the same day**. "Bins may not be collected at the same time each week or fortnight."
- No kerbside bulky item collection.
- [Noosa bin collection days](https://www.noosa.qld.gov.au/Environment-and-Waste/Waste-and-Recycling/Bin-collection-days)
- Fortnightly garden waste for urban residents, optional in some rural areas. A food-scraps collection trial runs at **Peppers Noosa Resort & Villas during 2026** with the Council of Mayors (SEQ), funded by the Queensland Government — [Noosa Garden & Compost](https://www.noosa.qld.gov.au/Environment-and-Waste/Waste-and-Recycling/Garden-and-Compost) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- A three-bin system is the norm, with general waste weekly and recycling and garden alternating fortnightly. Most households therefore put out **two bins every week**, which suits a weekly valet. Collection times vary and Noosa requires bins off the kerb the same day, both of which add to demand for a reliable bring-in service, especially at holiday rentals with turnover-day guests.
- No council has announced a move to fortnightly general waste. Elsewhere in Australia, councils have sometimes paired a future FOGO rollout with a switch to fortnightly red-bin collection. If that happened here, food waste would move into the green or lime bin and need cleaning more often (an odour and maggot problem). It would also change valet scheduling. This is a medium-term scenario, not announced locally.
- The FOGO trial targets **retirement communities and resorts, Bin Caddie's core segments**. These sites will be the first to deal with food-waste bins, caddies and extra bin-handling.
- The SCC lid colour change from green to red may cause temporary confusion for guests, a small talking point for a valet service.

### Gaps
- No date was found for an SCC or Noosa household FOGO rollout.
- The contents of the SCC "Food organics trial" page were not fetched.
- The current SCC general waste and recycling charges in rates (amounts) were not retrieved.
- No confirmation was found of any planned change from weekly to fortnightly general waste in either council.

## Do councils offer free assisted bin services that compete with bin valet offerings?

### Takeaway
No assisted or infirm bin service was found on Sunshine Coast Council's or Noosa Council's bin pages. The fetched pages do not mention one, and targeted searches returned only other councils' programs. Many Queensland councils (Gold Coast, Toowoomba, Cairns, Lockyer Valley) do offer free medically-certified assisted services, and **Gold Coast also sells a paid wheel-out/wheel-back service to all residents**, a direct council competitor model.

### Cited Findings
- The SCC general waste page makes no mention of an assisted collection service — [SCC general waste](https://www.sunshinecoast.qld.gov.au/living-and-community/waste-and-recycling/bin-collection-services/general-waste-bin-collection)
- The SCC bin services hub does not mention an assisted service — [SCC bin collection services](https://www.sunshinecoast.qld.gov.au/living-and-community/waste-and-recycling/bin-collection-services)
- Targeted searches for "assisted" or "medical certificate" found no SCC page — [SCC online waste forms](https://www.sunshinecoast.qld.gov.au/council/contact-council/council-documents/waste-and-recycling/online-waste-forms) (search summary)
- Noosa bin collection page: no mention of assisted service — [Noosa bin collection days](https://www.noosa.qld.gov.au/Environment-and-Waste/Waste-and-Recycling/Bin-collection-days)
- Gold Coast council:
  - **Assisted household bin service** for residents physically unable to move bins. Council first asks whether family, carers or neighbours can help, and requires written support from a medical professional.
  - **Fee-paying wheel-out, wheel-back service open to all residents** ("Fees apply"; price not published).
  - [City of Gold Coast — Need help with your bins?](https://www.goldcoast.qld.gov.au/Services/Waste-recycling/Residential-bins/Need-help-with-your-bins)
- Other Queensland examples:
  - Toowoomba requires a doctor's certificate, with bins within 40m of the service point — [Toowoomba RC infirm services](https://www.tr.qld.gov.au/environment-water-waste/waste-recycling/household-waste-collection-wheelie-bins/9877-infirm-services)
  - Cairns requires a medical practitioner's assessment, with service set up within about 10 working days — [Cairns RC form](https://www.cairns.qld.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0011/469640/Applicaton-for-new-Infirm-Service.pdf)
  - Lockyer Valley offers a free service where there is no able-bodied person at the address and the contractor collects from within the property — [Lockyer Valley RC](https://www.lockyervalley.qld.gov.au:443/our-services/waste-management/wheelie-bins-and-waste-collection/request-waste-collection-assistance)
  - (All via search summaries)

### Inferences
- Council assisted schemes in Queensland are typically **narrowly targeted**: a medical certificate is required and no able-bodied household member or helper can be available. They do not serve holiday rentals, absentee owners or body corporates, so they do not compete with Bin Caddie's core market. They could compete for the frail-resident segment.
- Absent evidence of an SCC or Noosa assisted scheme, residents with limited mobility may rely on family, neighbours or paid services. This is a possible residential niche, but it needs verification.
- The Gold Coast paid wheel-out service shows a council could, in principle, offer a paid valet. No sign of this was found on the Sunshine Coast.

### Gaps
- **Not verified:** whether SCC or Noosa provide an assisted or infirm service through their collection contractors, unpublished or by phone request. Recommend calling SCC (07) 5475 7272 and Noosa Council to confirm. No reliable source either way was found.
- Gold Coast's wheel-out/wheel-back fee amount is not published online.

## What are typical economics and models for bin cleaning/valet businesses in Australia (equipment, pricing, franchises)?

### Takeaway
Australian residential bin cleaning is a low-ticket, route-density business, at about **$8–$17 per bin per clean** on recurring plans and $15–$40 once-off. Valet is priced monthly at roughly **$80–$160/month per property**. Equipment ranges from about **$20k (used trailer) to $37k inc. GST (new trailer) to $121k inc. GST (truck system)**. Franchise entry for bin cleaning was reported at about $50–75k total, but this figure is unverified.

### Cited Findings
**Pricing norms (recurring cleaning)**
- ChuteOff $10 fortnightly, $12 monthly, $15 once-off per bin — [ChuteOff](https://www.chuteoff.com.au/bin-cleaning/)
- It's Bin Cleaned about $8–10 per bin on 3–6 month contracts — [It's Bin Cleaned](https://itsbincleaned.com.au/) (search summary)
- Proud Bin Cleaning $12.50 recurring, $27.50 as-required — [Proud Bin Cleaning](https://www.proudbincleaning.com.au/wheelie-bin-cleaning-prices.html) (search summary)
- Col's Bin Cleaning (Sutherland Shire, NSW) from $6 per bin, 4-weekly — [Oneflare: Col's Bin Cleaning](https://www.oneflare.com.au/b/col-s-bin-cleaning) (search summary)
- Turbo's from $15 per bin — [Turbo's](https://turboswheelieclean.com.au/booking/)
- Bin Caddie from $17 — [Bin Caddie](https://www.bincaddie.com.au/bin-cleaning)

**Pricing norms (valet)**
- The Bin Butler (Gold Coast) $160/month inc. GST, up to 4 bins, cleaning included — [The Bin Butler](https://thebinbutler.com/bin-valet/)
- Bin Caddie $80/month ex GST holiday home, from $100/month ex GST body corporate — [Bin Caddie](https://www.bincaddie.com.au/bin-placement)

**Operating model norms**
- Cleaners clean at the kerb on bin day after the council truck empties the bin, so routes follow council collection-day zones.
- If bins aren't out, Turbo's charges 50% of the fee — [Turbo's FAQ](https://turboswheelieclean.com.au/frequently-asked-questions/)
- Self-contained water and power, with about 90% wash-water recapture (The Bin Butler) — [The Bin Butler](https://thebinbutler.com/bin-valet/)
- "Legal water disposal" (ChuteOff) — [ChuteOff](https://www.chuteoff.com.au/bin-cleaning/)

**Equipment costs**
- Sprintjett (Australian supplier): bin cleaning trailer $34,000 ex GST ($37,400 inc.), truck system $110,000 ex GST ($121,000 inc.) — [Sprintjett bin cleaning equipment](https://sprintjett.com.au/product-category/bin-cleaning/) (via search summary)
- A used wheelie-bin-cleaning trailer business listed in Byron Bay at $20,000 asking — [BusinessForSale: Byron Bay](https://www.businessforsale.com.au/australia/772eace2/wheelie-bin-cleaning) (via search summary)
- Lower-capacity mains-pressure units (BinBlaster BB1000) — [Emoveit BinBlaster](https://emoveit.com.au/product/bin-blaster-mobile-wheelie-bin-washer) (via search summary)

**Franchises**
- Oz Bin Cleaning reportedly costs $50,000–$75,000 in total, with a franchise fee of about $25,000, royalties of about 6–8% of gross, and liquid capital of $25–30k. This comes only from a search snippet. The listing page (franchisewisely.com.au) is now a parked domain, so the figure is **unverified** — [Franchise Wisely (parked)](https://franchisewisely.com.au/franchise/oz-bin-cleaning/)
- It's Bin Cleaned appears as a franchise on Franchise Insights, but its cost breakdown shows only $5,000–15,000 in legal fees, with other lines at $0, so it is unreliable — [Franchise Insights: Its Bin Cleaned](https://franchiseinsights.com.au/franchise/its-bin-cleaned/cost) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- **Bin cleaning economics (illustrative, not sourced):** at about $12–15 per bin and two bins per stop, revenue is about $25–30 per stop. Route density on the same collection-day zone drives profitability, as does fitting many stops in the window after the council truck passes.
- **Valet economics:** valet is labour and travel per stop (twice per bin day: out and back), with no equipment beyond a vehicle. Bin Caddie's $80 ex GST per month is about $18–20 per property per week. Assuming two short visits per week, margins depend on clustering properties by collection day and suburb. Body corporate sites with many bins per stop are the most efficient.
- Bundling valet with cleaning raises revenue per stop. The valet operator is already on site after collection when bins are emptied, which is the cleaning window, so the two services share the same visit.
- **Bin Caddie's equipment is not described.** If it uses a smaller mobile unit rather than a $37k+ trailer, capex is low, but cleaning capacity is too.

### Gaps
- No verified Australian franchise disclosure figures for a bin cleaning or valet franchise.
- No sourced revenue, margin or customer-per-day benchmarks for Australian bin cleaners.
- No industry body or market-size data for Australian bin cleaning was found.

## Supplementary local demand context (demographics, holiday rentals, climate)

### Cited Findings
- **Sunshine Coast LGA demographics (2021 Census):**
  - Median age 43, two years older than Regional Qld. Range from 29 (Baringa–Nirimba area, i.e. the Aura estate area) to 58 (Maleny–Witta–North Maleny) — [Atlas.id Sunshine Coast median age](https://atlas.id.com.au/sunshine-coast/median-age) (via search summary)
  - **21.7% aged 65+** (up from 20.3% in 2016), against 16.2% for SEQ — [profile.id / atlas.id aged 65+](https://atlas.id.com.au/sunshine-coast/aged-65-years-and-over) (via search summary)
  - The 70–74 age group grew most, up 33.9% since 2016 — [REMPLAN Sunshine Coast age](https://app.remplan.com.au/sunshine-coast/community/population/age) (via search summary)
- **Short-term rentals (estimates conflict by boundary and method):**
  - About 4,785 active Airbnb listings on the Sunshine Coast in March 2026, including 1,697 in Maroochy and 866 in Caloundra — [BnB Management Sunshine Coast blog, 2026](https://www.bnbmanagementsunshinecoast.com.au/sunshine-coast-57-occupancy-2026/) (operator blog; via search summary)
  - AirROI: 2,933 active listings for Sunshine Coast Regional, with supply down 13.2% year on year — [AirROI](https://www.airroi.com/airbnb-data/australia/queensland/sunshine-coast-regional) (via search summary)
  - AirDNA: 9,201 properties across Airbnb and Vrbo, 57% occupancy, $276 average daily rate — [AirDNA](https://www.airdna.co/vacation-rental-data/app/au/queensland/sunshine-coast/overview) (via search summary)
  - Inside Airbnb: about 7,297 listings, date unclear — [Inside Airbnb Sunshine Coast](https://insideairbnb.com/sunshine-coast/) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- An ageing population (over a fifth aged 65+, with fast growth in the 70–74 group) and thousands of short-stay rentals support both of Bin Caddie's demand pools. Young new estates (Aura/Baringa, median age 29) are less natural valet markets but could suit cleaning subscriptions.
- The Sunshine Coast's hot, humid subtropical summers plausibly make odour and maggot problems in weekly red bins and FOGO bins a seasonal driver of cleaning demand. Bin Caddie's own copy references "maggots, and odours that attract pests" — [Bin Caddie bin cleaning](https://www.bincaddie.com.au/bin-cleaning).
