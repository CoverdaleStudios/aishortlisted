## Q1. What services does Bin Caddie offer, and how is each described?

### Takeaway
Bin Caddie (trading as "Bin Caddie Property Services") is a B2B-leaning "bin management" business for **holiday rentals/Airbnbs and body corporates/strata**. Its three core services are **Bin Placement (bins in/out)**, **Bin Cleaning** and **Rubbish Removal**. Placement is the flagship, recurring, monthly-priced product. Cleaning is delivered in partnership with Sunshine Coast operator **Turbo's Wheelie Clean**.
