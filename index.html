The problem

AI search is roughly thirty times more selective than Google

Google's local pack shows you a list. An AI assistant gives an answer — one short list of names, with no second page and no "see more results". Either you're in it or you don't exist at that moment.

SOCi analysed more than 350,000 locations across 2,751 multi-location brands. ChatGPT recommended just 1.2% of them; Gemini 11%; Perplexity 7.4% — against an average 35.9% appearance rate in Google's local 3-pack. Locations that did get recommended averaged 4.3-star ratings. (Study covers multi-location brands; independent businesses were not measured — which is exactly why we measure yours directly.)

Most business owners have simply never checked. It is not on any dashboard they own, and their SEO reports do not mention it.

Questions

Straight answers

Is this exactly what ChatGPT shows my customers?

No, and we won't pretend otherwise. We query AI models through their APIs with live web search enabled. That's a strong, repeatable measure of how AI systems describe your market and who they favour — but the consumer apps add personalisation, memory, location signals and their own instructions, and answers vary between runs. We report what we measured and label it clearly.

Can you guarantee you'll get me recommended?

No. Anyone who guarantees an AI ranking is guessing. What we can do is measure your current position precisely, show you exactly which sources the AI reads, and tell you which of those you're missing from. What you do with that is the part that moves the number.

Does this matter for my industry?

It matters most where people research before they buy — cosmetic and dental clinics, specialists, legal and financial services, high-value trades. It matters least for genuine emergencies, where people just open Maps. If you're not sure, run the free check; it costs you nothing and the answer is specific to you.

What do I actually do with the report?

Most of the actions are things you or your web person can do in an afternoon: claim and correct listings on the specific directories we name, publish FAQ content matching the real questions we found, and add the structured data we generate for you. We give you the files ready to paste.
