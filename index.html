Questions

Is this exactly what ChatGPT shows my customers?

No, and we won't pretend otherwise. We query AI models through their APIs with live web search enabled. That's a strong, repeatable measure of how AI systems describe your market and who they favour. The consumer apps add personalisation, memory and location signals, and answers vary between runs. We report what we measured and label it clearly.

Can you guarantee you'll get me recommended?

No. Anyone who guarantees an AI ranking is guessing. What we can do is measure your current position precisely, show you exactly which sources the AI reads, and tell you which of those you're missing from. Acting on that is the part that moves the number.

Does this matter for my industry?

It matters most where people research before they buy: cosmetic and dental clinics, specialists, legal and financial services, high-value trades. It matters least for genuine emergencies, where people just open Maps. If you're not sure, run the free check. It costs nothing and the answer is specific to you.

What do I actually do with the report?

Most actions take you or your web person an afternoon: claim and correct the specific directories we name, publish content answering the real questions we found, and paste in the structured data we generate for you. The files come ready to use.
