# Noosa sign survey: mapping which agency manages which street

**Why:** every approved Noosa short-stay property must show a sign at the front, in letters at least 50 mm high, with its approval number and its 24/7 local contact person. The contact is usually a letting agency, so one drive tells you which agencies dominate which streets. That shows which Tier A account to call first and lets you open with "we're already on your street".

**When:** on a normal Bin Caddie run in Noosa Heads, Noosaville or Sunshine Beach. About 2 hours covers 15–25 streets. If no run goes there yet, use the `SB1` drop zone.

## How

1. Phone camera, or a dashcam with **audio switched off**. Only photograph from the footpath or road.
2. For each sign, photograph **the sign only**. Avoid people, faces, number plates and the inside of properties.
3. In the Workbook *Noosa Sign Log* tab, record:
   - date and street;
   - house number;
   - approval number;
   - **contact type**: `agency` or `individual`;
   - **agency name**, only if it's a business;
   - your notes.
4. **If the contact is an individual,** write `individual` and **don't record their name or number.** That phone line is for neighbour complaints, not marketing. Never call, text or email it.
5. Delete the photos once they're logged, within 7 days at most.

## Afterwards

- Total the agencies by street (Workbook → *Noosa Sign Log* → summary at the top).
- Any agency **not** already in *Accounts* gets added, with its published business email found on its own website.
- Add a line to that agency's email: "We counted [N] of your signs on [Street] / [Suburb] last week. We're already on those streets on [collection day]."
  - That's fine for an agency, because it refers to the agency's own public signage.
  - **Never use this approach with individual owners.**

## What not to do

- Don't knock on doors or leave notes in the letterboxes of signed properties during this survey. Letterbox drops are a separate task.
- Don't mention bins being left out in any message to anyone.
- Don't keep video. Stills only, deleted after logging.
