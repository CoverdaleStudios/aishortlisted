# Call script: manager follow-up (about 2 minutes)

**Compliance:**
- Business landlines only. Wash any mobile against the Do Not Call Register first.
- Weekdays 9am–8pm, Saturdays 9am–5pm, never Sundays. Check public holidays with a lawyer.
- Give your name and business at the start, and don't withhold your caller ID.
- If they say "don't call again", log it in *Suppression* immediately.

---

**Open (say who you are first):**
"Hi, it's [Name] from Bin Caddie, a local bin service on the coast. I emailed [contact or the team] on [day] about putting bins out at your holiday homes. Is [contact] the right person for property operations?"

*If no:* "Who looks after things like cleaners and maintenance for your rentals?" → note the name and role → "Could I send them a short email?"

**Hook (one sentence):**
"We put guest bins out before collection and back in after, twice a week, with a time-stamped photo every visit. Most managers we talk to have the cleaners juggling it or neighbours complaining."

**Question (then listen):**
"How do you handle bins at the [N] homes you manage at the moment?"

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Cleaners do it" | "Does that work on collection days when there's no changeover? We go twice a week whether there's a guest or not." |
| "Guests do it" | "How often does that get missed? Each miss is a full bin for the next guest and sometimes a neighbour complaint." |
| "Owners do it" | "We can bill the owner directly or through you, whichever is easier. You'd just get the photos." |
| "Too expensive" | "It's $80 a month per home ex GST for up to four bins. Many managers pass that on to the owner as a line item. Want to trial it free on a few homes first?" |
| "Not now" | "No problem. Can I check back after [peak season / Christmas]?" → log the date |

**Close:**
"Could we trial it on 3 to 5 homes for two weeks, free **(confirm)**? You pick the ones that give you the most bin trouble. I just need the addresses and collection days."

**Log it:** outcome, next step, date → *Outreach Log*.
