# Phase 0 outreach: three angles, tested on 15 managers

**How to use this:** send each angle to 5 managers (15 total). Pick the angle from the `pitch` and `angle` columns in `Phase0_Workbook.xlsx` → *Accounts*. Send from a real Bin Caddie mailbox, one email at a time, personalised. At this volume, no automation tool is needed.

**Before every send, check the Spam Act rules:**
- [ ] The address was published by the business (its `email_source_url` is filled in).
- [ ] The source page has no "no marketing" notice.
- [ ] The email is about their role. Bin management for a letting manager is.
- [ ] The footer below is included, unchanged.
- [ ] Anyone who replies "unsubscribe" or "stop" goes into the *Suppression* tab the same day, and is never emailed again.

Placeholders in `[BRACKETS]` must be filled in. Bin Caddie must confirm anything marked **(confirm)**.

## Angle A: "Guest bins are a job nobody owns"
*Best for: large holiday-home portfolios (houses ≥ units), e.g. Welcome Ready, Aspire, Holiday Homes @ Noosa, Belle Escapes.*

**Subject options:** `Bins at your [Suburb] homes` · `Who puts the bins out at [N] holiday homes?`

> Hi [First name or "[Business] team"],
>
> Quick one: who puts the wheelie bins out and brings them back in at your [N] holiday homes around [top suburbs]?
>
> For most managers it's a cleaner squeezing it in, a guest who forgets, or a neighbour who complains. We're Bin Caddie, a local family business that only does this. We put bins out before council collection and bring them back in after, twice a week. Every visit gets a time- and location-stamped photo, so you can show owners it was done.
>
> It's $80 a month per home (ex GST) for up to 4 bins, with no lock-in. We'd like to prove it on [3–5] of your homes with a free 2-week trial **(confirm)**. Pick the properties that give you the most bin trouble.
>
> Worth a 10-minute chat this week?
>
> [Sender name]
> [Footer]

**Follow-up 1 (day 4):**
> Hi [First name], following up in case this got buried. Happy to send a sample of the photo report owners would see, so you can judge it before any trial. Would that help? [Sender]

**Follow-up 2 (day 10, last one):**
> Hi [First name], I won't keep chasing. If bins ever become a headache, such as a complaint, a missed collection or an overflowing bin at changeover, we're on 0418 475 510 and can usually start within a week. All the best for the season. [Sender]

## Angle B: "Noosa contact person" (Noosa managers only)
*Best for: Noosa-based agencies that act as the 24/7 local contact person: Niche Holidays Noosa, Penny In Noosa, Noosa Beach Abodes, Zinc, Host Angel, Accom Noosa, Laguna, Noosa Holiday Rentals.*

> ⚠️ Don't say there is a Noosa council rule about bins until it has been checked against the **current** short-stay approval conditions. Phase 0 task 3 in `README.md` covers that check. Until then, use the wording below, which relies only on verified facts.

**Subject:** `One less complaint for your Noosa contact line`

> Hi [First name],
>
> As a Noosa short-stay manager you're the contact on the property sign and have to respond to complaints within 30 minutes. Bins left on the kerb or sitting full after a big changeover are among the easiest complaints to prevent.
>
> Bin Caddie puts bins out before collection and brings them back in after, twice a week, across Noosa Heads, Noosaville, Sunshine Beach and surrounds. Every visit gets a time-stamped photo, so if a neighbour does complain, you have proof straight away.
>
> $80 per home per month ex GST, no lock-in. Could we trial it free on [3–5] of your properties for 2 weeks **(confirm)**? You choose which ones.
>
> [Sender name]
> [Footer]

**Follow-ups:** use Angle A's follow-ups, replacing "owners" with "neighbours and council".
